### Mathematical Observations & Root Cause of Compression
1. **Severe Score Compression**: In the current Synastry V1 engine, **99.94% of all random human pairs fall between 50% and 69%**. Scores below 50% and scores above 75% literally never occur in natural human populations.
2. **The Law of Large Numbers in Synastry**: Each synastry chart compares 10 bodies $\times$ 10 bodies = 100 cross-aspect combinations. While individual planetary aspects have high variance (e.g. Venus-Mars opposition vs trine), the composite score averages all 100 interactions. As predicted by the Central Limit Theorem, the sum of 100 semi-independent variables has low standard deviation ($\sigma \approx 2.2\%$).
3. **High Inherent Baseline**: The Synastry V1 specification sets the neutral baseline at 50%. Even pairs with severe square/opposition tensions still have neutral or mildly supportive minor aspects, pulling the total score right back toward ~55%–58%.
4. **Product Implication**: A consumer seeing their best friend get 63% and their worst nemesis get 54% perceives JESTER as "broken" or "flat" because humans expect a scale of 10% to 95%.

## 3. Calibration Proposals (Non-Invasive Calibration Layer)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The underlying astrology engine (coordinates, orb logic, quadratic decay, weights, symmetry) remains **completely locked and untouched**.
> The calibration layer is a monotonic, deterministic mathematical mapping:
> $$\text{Astrology Engine} \longrightarrow \text{Raw Score } (x) \longrightarrow \text{Calibration Function } f(x) \longrightarrow \text{Display Index } (y)$$

#### Candidate 1: Logistic S-Curve (Sigmoid)
An inverted logit centered at empirical median $\mu = 58.3$:
$$f_1(x) = 12.0 + \frac{84.0}{1 + e^{-0.22(x - 58.3)}}$$
* **Characteristics**: Infinitely smooth, asymptotic bounds $[12\%, 96\%]$. Natural flattening at extremes.

#### Candidate 2: Power-Law Active Expansion
Normalizes the empirical active band $[50, 75]$ into $[0, 1]$ and applies sub-linear power expansion ($\gamma = 0.65$):
$$z = \text{clamp}\left(\frac{x - 50.0}{25.0}, 0, 1\right), \quad f_2(x) = 15.0 + 82.0 \cdot z^{0.65}$$
* **Characteristics**: Strongly pulls low scores down to 15%–25%, rapidly accelerates through the mid-range.

#### Candidate 3: Quantile-Aligned Piecewise Spline *(Recommended)*
A strictly monotonic piecewise linear function anchored directly to empirical percentiles:
* $x \le 50.0 \rightarrow y = 15.0\%$ *(Near theoretical floor)*

## 4. Hard JESTER Voice Architecture

### The Problem with the Previous Corpus
The original Georgian text suffered from:
1. **Polite Horoscope Flattery**: Phrases like *"ინტელექტუალური თავისუფლების რაინდი"*, *"ახლობლებისთვის ნამდვილი დამცავი ფარი ხარ"*, and *"გულუხვობა შენთვის ბუნებრივი ჟესტია"*.
2. **Predictable Sentence Templates**: 80%+ of assets began with *"ერთი შეხედვით..."*, *"მოდი პირდაპირ ვთქვათ..."*, or *"სინამდვილეში შენ..."*.
3. **Lack of Ego-Poking**: It described personality traits without exposing the coping mechanism, social vanity, or contradiction.

### The New JESTER Voice Rules
* **Core Triad Formula**:
  $$\text{OBSERVATION} \longrightarrow \text{CONTRADICTION} \longrightarrow \text{JESTER PUNCHLINE}$$
* **Tone Distribution**: Witty (35%), Playful (29%), Soft (18%), Bold (9%), Savage (8%), Romantic (1%).
* **Ego Poking**: Focus on how the person acts socially, what excuses they make for themselves, and where their logic fails.
* **Preservation of Semantic Fidelity**: When Venus-Mars signifies sexual chemistry or Sun-Saturn signifies responsibility, the punchline attacks the specific tension of that aspect rather than inventing unrelated traits.
* **Zero Astrology Jargon**: No mentions of *ორბი, ტრანზიტი, ასცენდენტი, პლანეტა, ეკლიპტიკა*.
