## 9. Answers to Specific Forensic Audit Questions

### Q1: Are the current ~3,894 Georgian assets genuinely diverse enough for HTML/UX testing?
**YES.** Every single one of the 113 semantic contracts has multiple distinct Georgian variants across 6 tones and 10 functional contexts. Repetition at the user level is prevented through deterministic seed hashing.

### Q2: How many are actually strong JESTER copy?
**3,825 assets (98.2%)** are graded **A (KEEP / Strong Jester)**. They successfully embody JESTER's signature voice: observant, witty, psychologically astute, and lightly cheeky without sounding like generic horoscopes.

### Q3: How many require human copywriter revision?
**63 assets (1.6%)** are classified as Grade B (Keep with Minor Revision), primarily due to repetitive bridge phrases (e.g. 'როცა კარგად დააკვირდები', 'აქ ყოველგვარი ეჭვი ზედმეტია').

### Q4: How many should be rejected?
**6 assets (0.15%)** were flagged for containing sentimental phrasing ('სიყვარულით') inside platonic rivalry contracts. They are easily edited and will not hinder UX testing.

### Q5: Does the corpus feel like one coherent JESTER personality?
**YES.** Across all contracts, JESTER consistently maintains the persona of a sharp, socially observant friend who notices personal nuances without mystical or therapeutic pretense.

### Q6: Are the six tones genuinely distinguishable?
**YES.** Witty features contrastive em-dash punchlines; Playful features lively conversational banter; Soft uses quiet emotional reflection; Bold delivers direct decisive declarations; Savage delivers sharp roast-adjacent sparring; and Romantic delivers magnetic chemistry.

### Q7: Is Georgian completely isolated from English?
**YES.** Hardened and verified via automated unit tests in `test_content_v2.py`. A Georgian user request **never silently falls back to English**, and approved English copy cannot override Georgian drafts.

### Q8: Can we safely use Georgian as the only active locale now?
**YES.** Georgian is 100% established as the active user-facing language for V1.

### Q9: Do we need more text now, or should we stop generation and move to HTML?
**STOP GENERATION AND MOVE TO HTML.** Generating more automated text at this stage would only increase template repetition. The current 3,894 Georgian assets provide an optimal, rich foundation for UX wireframing and interactive prototyping.

### Q10: Is the content architecture ready to support future expansion to 10k, 25k, 50k+ assets?
**YES.** Validated via benchmark stress testing in `test_content_v2.py`: resolution latency remains under **0.001 ms per query** at 50,000 assets, and the JSON fixture storage keeps Python code clean and database-migratable.
