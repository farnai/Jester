## Art direction
Editorial Dialogue combines the first supplied reference’s tactile material study, the second’s confident photographic sequencing, and the third’s asymmetric editorial hierarchy. Chrome and carrot-orange quotation sculptures make conversation the graphic focus. Sage observation, charcoal relationship story, portrait-led discovery and orange invitation vary section rhythm.

## Content boundary
All people, portraits, activity and interpretation examples are synthetic illustration for this concept. Portraits and sculpture were generated with pen.dev AI. Daily observation explicitly says it derives from the profile; this is not a claim of live daily transit capability. No private birth information or compatibility scores were fabricated. Adult-only footer retained. Navigation reflects the five established destinations. Prototype is static; mobile bottom navigation is shown at the end of a full-page presentation and intended to be pinned in implementation.
